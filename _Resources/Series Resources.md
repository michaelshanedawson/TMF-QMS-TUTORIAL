# Before I Begin #
I am not sponsored yet by any of the companies listed below. They are all derived from my professional experience in the field and have been proven reliable and repeatable. While using sources such as Amazon are viable and great for low volume prototypes, I do not recommend for long term planning. Cost savings are good for starting out but long term planning requires the ability to plan for a long life cycle for the product you are manufacturing, at least 5 years out. THat sort of life cycle calls for stability. It is also easier to get CofCs for the products you order to verify that they do meet all requirements.

# Mechanical Parts Vendors #
* McMaster-Carr - www.mcmaster.com - Great all around source for fasteners and other hardware for products. Fast shipping and great customer support.
* MiSUMi - us.misumi-ec.com - Great source for many different components. I like that they offer round O-Ring style belts in custom lengths, they make them to order.

# EDM Software #
* Altium Designer - www.altium.com - A very robust package with a lot of features to support companies of all sizes. Just has a higher cost to entry compared to other options but it is worth the expense due to the included features.
* KiCad - www.kicad.com - Free Schematic and PCB capture application
* Circuit Studio - Apparently it will be renamed and upgraded to CircuitMaker, will just need to wait until their refresh cycle is completed. Indicators show it to have a free and paid "Pro" version. Existing customers with Circuit Studio will apparently be upgraded to the Pro version at no charge.
* Eagle - Packaged inside of Fusion now, subscription based.
* Allegro X - www.cadence.com - Very capable but also high cost.

Many others are out there but I would recommend a lot of research before settling on any package.

# PCB vendors #
* JLCPCB - www.jlcpcb.com - Low and high volume supplier, low cost for proto batch sizes. I prefer their full sized and framed stencils. Fast turn around time for small quantities. They can handle assembly as well for those looking to outsource manufacturing.
* PCBWay - www.pcbway.com - Low and high volume supplier, low cost for proto batch sizes. Fast turn around time for small quantities. They can handle assembly as well for those looking to outsource manufacturing.
* OSH Park - www.oshpark.com - Low and high volume supplier, low cost for proto batch sizes. Fast turn around time for small quantities. I am not a fan of their board quality, boards already violate IPC class 1 requirements when shipped as they don't clean up the board edges and leave sharp points from when they were cut from the fabrication panels.
* Sierra Circuits - www.protoexpress.com - Better for large volumes, not cost friendly to small batches. They can handle assembly as well, US based. Great quality and a great support staff.

# SMT Supplies Vendors #
* Hisco - www.hisco.com -  Good selection and pricing for bench top equipment.
* Horizon Sales - www.horizonsales.com - Great selection and prices. They also deal in manufacturing equipment and can help setup a full production lines. Generally new equipment though. I have worked with them in the past, very helpful and friendly staff. Great for large quantity pricing and supply of consumables such as solder wire and paste.

# SMT Equipment Vendors #
## Pre Owned ##
* PPM - www.goppm.com - Great customer service, specializes in older equipment. They bought out the IP to the Tyco Quad series of P&P machines and supply replacement parts for the associated equipment. They have been actively making improvements to the old machines such as newer control systems. The Quad machines may be old but they are reliable and if taken care of properly they are great starter machines for an up and coming company.
