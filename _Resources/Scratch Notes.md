Ripple voltage on the 3.3V supply @ 2A loaded

Using Ohms law, we ned to determine the resistance needed to get the PS to source the 2A and the power dissipated from that resistor so we can size a test unit properly.

R=V/I
R=3.3/2
R=1.65Ω - Factor in tolerance of test resistor = +/- 20%

P=V*I
P=3.3*2
P=6.6W for reference, an 0603 resistor can handle a max power of 100mW or 0.1W
