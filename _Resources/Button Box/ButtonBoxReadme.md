## 16mm Button Box With Lid ##
* Designed to fit a single 16mm button commonly found in automation equipment.
* Lid held down by M3 heat set inserts and M3 button head screws, 4mm to 6mm in length are sufficient.
* Outlet hole will accept a 3 conductor cable or 4 plain wires 22AWG in size.
* STL and Fusion files provided, feel free to use for your projects.
* Prints well in PLA and easy to open the button hole more with a deburring tool.
