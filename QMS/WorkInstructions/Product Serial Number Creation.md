Product serial numbers shall contain the following information:

* Product Number, just the numerical components
* Date code in Julians format, can use this calculator for it: https://calculator-online.net/julians-date-calculator/
* Manufacturing Order Number for the Assembly level, just the numerical components
* Device sequence starting at 1.

datecode-mfg#-device sequence

Example:
product P1012202501, manufactured today would be 1/10/2026 converted to Julians would be 26010, mfg# would be 3, sequence is 1.
Convert each entry item to hex value

The serial # would be:
3C54FC05-659A-3-1


Dates do not have to be exact, if the manufacturing pauses over a shutdown, no new labels are required. If labels are printed already but the build is delayed, an allowance of a single work week of difference is allowed before new labels must be printed. If a job has to be paused due to part concerns, no new labels will be required to be printed.

Serial numbers would also get registered in the associated manufacturing log for the work order.
